# Objective, delayed effects and cost provenance

Research hypothesis: choosing a useful next investigation under a budget can reduce
time to independently actionable escalation compared with strong deterministic
orchestration. Trace evidence might change subsequent Diagnose/escalation choices;
repeated low-value calls might consume the deadline. These are untested causal claims.
The current repository does not provide measured first-responder transition dynamics.

Proposed primary endpoint is elapsed time from trusted detection to independently
adjudicated actionable escalation, with missed escalations retained as censored or
failure outcomes under a preregistered horizon. Pair it with correct escalation rate;
otherwise never-escalate or always-escalate strategies can game a time metric.
A future protocol must fix which endpoint is primary and its exact scoring before
comparison, including competing events, censoring and non-actionable incidents.
No numerical weights, deadlines or success thresholds are approved here.

Useful evidence requires an independent adjudicator linking an artifact to a correct
resource/cause or a decision that becomes actionable under the scenario rubric.
A finding count, confident text, role success or analyst acceptance is insufficient.
Reward channels could include adjudicated evidence value, correct escalation and
resource identification. Until rubric reliability and provenance are demonstrated,
there is no implemented scalar reward and no training-ready objective.

Keep the following costs separately reported with raw units and missingness:
charged USD/tokens/tool calls from reconciled ledgers; queue/provider/execution latency
from trusted job clocks; analyst interruption count/time from approved notification
and acknowledgement records; false/missed/delayed escalation from independent truth;
failures from terminal statuses; unsupported recommendations from capability/scope
checks; workload from active/pending job accounting. Caps are independent constraints,
not penalties a policy may trade for reward. No fabricated measured-zero defaults.

Legitimate-user disruption and attack harm reduction require independently measured
application effects, actual authorized control execution and a defensible comparison.
This fixture-only proposal cannot measure them. Recommendation accuracy may be scored
against an independent rubric, but a recommendation is not containment. A patch
suggestion is not remediation; only independent verification supports that claim.
Security claims in agent output never settle containment outcomes.

A future constrained objective may maximize expected independently adjudicated utility
subject to per-incident scope, disclosure, cost, latency, workload and safety limits.
Discounting, horizon, weights and constraint thresholds need preregistration. Do not
reuse payment-read or resource-availability thresholds for this distinct task. Model
selection may use training/validation only; evaluator-only outcomes stay sealed.
