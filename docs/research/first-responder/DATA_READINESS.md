# Training-data readiness audit

This is a read-only source/model audit, not a production database census or formal
collection. No attested incident dataset was supplied. Absence of that dataset does
not establish that the product has zero incidents. Static source inventory and unit
fixtures are not incident trajectories, live evidence or counterfactual training data.

Canonical AgentRun and AgentStepResult support role/run/assessment identity, terminal
status, generic validated result envelope, provider mode, model/prompt identity,
artifacts, tool count and Usage. Existing role runners validate specialized outputs.
Usage defaults are not measured zero; sandbox seconds are not detection-to-escalation
latency. These records do not alone establish complete predecision masks, behavior
probabilities, contemporaneous incident context or independent downstream usefulness.
Replay/fake/live must be retained and never pooled as equivalent measured evidence.

Historical findings and independent verification support code vulnerability/fix
claims at pinned versions. A vulnerability is not proof of a live attack or a useful
first-responder decision. SnapshotRef supports commit-specific intelligence; current
production deployment binding must be established separately. Existing owned target
fixtures support unit invariants only unless a separately scoped incident collection
protocol is approved. Historical payment-read/resource/v6/v10 traces have different
state/action/outcome semantics and cannot supply first-responder policy comparisons.

Existing Live Defense telemetry and recommendations may support event normalization
and recommendation provenance when actually captured. Shadow NOT_EXECUTED records
cannot prove containment. Analyst accept/dismiss can inform feedback but not replace
independent ground truth. Incident persistence and approval models do not establish
that new Grok records, capability masks or outcome producers already exist.
Grok's actual first-responder contract remains pending exact branch/PR and review.

## Required trajectory separation

A future approved dataset needs incident/scenario identity and split; behavior policy
and version; immutable decision-time observation/cutoffs and feasible actions; selected
proposal and actual behavior probability if known; invocation request/run and terminal
artifact; subsequent effects; notification/delivery/analyst response; independent
outcome and adjudicator provenance, each with its own availability. Include incomplete,
failed, timed-out, cancelled, benign and unsafe cases. Retain missingness, revisions,
rejected actions and unexecuted recommendations. Never retrospectively insert outcomes
into decision input. Watermarks must establish aggregate completeness.

Deterministic logs have no support for actions never chosen. Unknown propensities
cannot be invented for inverse-propensity scoring. Observational correlations between
agent calls and good outcomes do not identify treatment effect; confounding by incident
severity is expected. Offline RL/counterfactual ranking is unsupported without action
coverage, validated dynamics or a separately justified experimental design.

Readiness gates currently unmet: agreed actual producer contract; trusted deployment
binding and availability journal; charge reconciliation; independent outcome rubric
and inter-rater checks; authorized complete dataset and consent/retention policy;
strong frozen deterministic baseline; faithful environment or justified owned effects
study; prespecified splits, samples, limits and failure rules. Therefore training is
NO-GO now, although bounded research specification receives CONDITIONAL GO.
