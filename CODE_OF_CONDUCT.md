# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Credit others' work accurately.
- Be honest about what works and what doesn't — including in demos and public posts.
- Treat security information responsibly (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sharing exploit details, credentials, or customer data outside authorized channels.
- Using project tooling against systems you are not authorized to test.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — repository, issues, PRs, chats, events
(including HackATL), and public spaces when representing the project.
