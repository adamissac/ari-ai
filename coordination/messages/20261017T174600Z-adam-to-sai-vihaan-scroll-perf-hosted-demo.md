- Summary (facts, not assumptions):
  - The user found momentum scrolling laggy. Measured in headless Chromium (software rendering, 120 wheel steps on `/`): the mean frame went from 82 ms to 21 ms. Causes and fixes:
    - `hero.tsx` wrote `<html>` data attributes on every scroll frame, which restyled the whole document. It now writes only on change and marks the hero `data-covered` once the page covers it, which stops painting and the orb animation.
    - The per-frame `filter: blur()` on `.hero-content` is gone, and the orbs are radial gradients instead of `blur(60px)` layers. The look is the same.
    - `useTrack` in `story.tsx` measures and re-renders only while its chapter intersects the viewport.
    - `.bleed` and `.hero-cover` use a `::after` background layer instead of 100vmax box-shadows. `.story-rain::before` is untouched.
    - `smooth-scroll.tsx` EASE goes from 0.085 to 0.11, which is slightly snappier.
  - Bug on main: `.hero-ink span { color: white }` also hit the label inside white buttons, so "Go to projects" was blank. Fixed with `.hero-ink :is(a, button).bg-white span { color: inherit }`.
  - `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_ONLY=1` (set only on the Vercel project): `/projects*` redirects to `/demo` and `/` renders without a session. When the flag is unset, nothing changes.
